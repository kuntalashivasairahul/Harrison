---
name: dataset-profiler
description: Use when inspecting corpus quality, chunk structure, metadata consistency, duplicates, source coverage, or ingestion quality in ML and RAG systems.
---

Use this skill to inspect the quality of indexed or source data.

Check for:
- duplicate or near-duplicate chunks
- malformed or sparse metadata
- skewed chunk sizes
- bad document parsing
- missing page/source attribution
- noisy tables or OCR artifacts
- uneven source coverage

Output:
1. Data quality findings
2. Likely downstream impact
3. Highest-priority cleanup actions
4. What to measure after cleanup
